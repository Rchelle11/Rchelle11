FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

What type of videos does RFC Studio create?

We create short-form videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts, including video editing, AI videos, product commercials, UGC-style content, promotional videos, and social media content.

Do I need to provide the video footage?

Not always. If you already have footage, we can edit it into engaging short-form content. If you don’t have footage, we can also create visuals using AI depending on your project requirements.

Can you create AI videos from scratch?

Yes. We can create AI-powered scenes, characters, environments, product visuals, and creative concepts based on your idea or brief.

Can you edit videos that I already have?

Absolutely. Send us your raw footage and we’ll handle the editing, captions, transitions, sound effects, music, pacing, and other creative elements needed for the final video.

How long does a video take?

Turnaround depends on the type, length, complexity, and number of videos required. We’ll discuss the timeline with you before starting the project.

Do you offer monthly or bulk packages?

Yes. If you need consistent content every week or month, we can create a package based on your content volume and requirements.

Can you follow our brand style?

Yes. You can provide your brand guidelines, colors, fonts, references, or previous content, and we’ll use them as a guide.

Can you create videos for paid ads?

Yes. We can create product videos, UGC-style content, AI commercials, and short-form videos designed for social media advertising.

What do you need from us to get started?

Usually, we’ll need your footage or product assets, project brief, brand information, references, and the main goal of the video.

Don’t have everything ready? That’s okay. We can discuss your idea first and figure out what you need.

How do I start a project with RFC Studio?

Simply contact us through the website. Tell us about your project, what type of video you need, and your requirements. We’ll get back to you with the next steps.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. RFC Studio can work with brands and clients remotely, regardless of location.
